I lead the Integrations team at Regie.ai. We connect Salesforce and HubSpot to Regie so customers can bring their data in and send updates back. I’ve recently been working on a monday.com integration too.

The whole sync architecture is fascinating to me. An import stops halfway through. Where do you pick it up again? How do you know an update actually made it to the other side? I design how the first import and later updates work, including how jobs recover when permissions change or a provider’s API does something unexpected. Each customer’s data has to stay separate through all of it.

I also spend time with customers to understand how they actually use their CRM. With Commvault, I worked with their leadership from onboarding through renewal. We went through how leads came in, who owned them and where automation handed things over to a person. Those conversations helped us figure out what we needed to change in the product and what their team needed help with.
